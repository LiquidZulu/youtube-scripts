Welcome to the unofficial Media Kit for Stop Killing Games!
This is a collection of all media, flyers, images etc. that have been constructed by volunteers so far. Feel free to make use of and modify these however you want to spread the word.
I encourage you to make your own materials, flyers, images, translations, etc. 
The flyer SVG is editable in Inkscape, there are docx files you can edit too, and the Mario Posters folder has a .pptx file with the source of most of the images. You'll need to install a font to display it properly, located in the zip file. Extract the files, then double click the .ttf file, then click the "Install" button to install the font. Due to the limits of NES characters, letters with accents might display strangely.


There are a couple of .txt files with ideas and links about the movement. skg common rebuttals.txt has the most amount of links and clips for combatting common anti-SKG talking points on the Internet.

There are subtitles to relevant Stop Killing Games videos that you can translate and modify. Use NotePad++ (or whatever text editor you use) and make sure there is a full line break between the subtitle number and the previous subtitle.

Send subtitles to danielsangeo on Discord to be added to a video. Otherwise, submit them to the subtitles forum on the Accursed Farms forum: https://www.accursedfarms.com/forums/forum/19-subtitles/

The Italian materials are translated by someone fluent in Italian (in the docx folder), but their written Italian is not perfect. If you are fluent in Italian, please correct the translations before spreading them around (except the Mario posters, those should be fine)!

The "videos & clips" folder contain videos that explain or expand upon SKG, some of which address detracting points. Feel free to spread them around!

If after everything, someone still disagrees with SKG, they may have a values clash, prioritize avoiding the government above stopping game destruction, or don't believe digital planned obsolescence is a problem.

I encourage anyone with substantive counter arguments (or supporters!) to contact Ross or the Initiative organizers, especially if you have an audience of some size or a way to one:
(click "Show more Info"): https://eci.ec.europa.eu/045/public/#/screen/home


Things ARE flexible, and the organizers are OPEN to hearing your ideas and having discussions 
See SKG Languages.png in the "images" folder for available languages and skg coverage & organizers.txt for the contact information of organizers and non-English coverage!

Refer to the datalake in #resources in the future for a more organized collection of links


I hope this makes an impact! Sorry for how long it took to update. Hopefully it will have been worth the wait. Thank you!

#+title: Pluribus

Thesis: you cannot have it half-way. If you give into the Hive and accept their gifts and accept them as a friendly presence, you fail. They have won. This is why it is important for the Hive to have this friendly face for you---they will lie to you, manipulate you, do whatever it is to make you feel the Collective as a warm blanket around you. And the second you do that, you have lost, and you will willingly join them. At that very moment, /you/ cease to exist. All there is is the egregore, and the games stop. You are just another cell of the collective body.
+ They own nothing, everything they have was stolen. By accepting gifts from them, you are implicitly acknowledging that they have a right to those things. That those bodies belong to the Hive, that that property belongs to the Hive, that the world belongs to the Hive.
